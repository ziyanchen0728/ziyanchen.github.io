Current independent products

AI-era product probes.

SoloCare, Solo HQ, and SoloSpeak are presented as living product experiments for HCI research framing and future user studies.

Care · Wellbeing · SafetyiOS

SoloCare

A quiet daily check-in app for people living alone. The design explores how small rituals, trusted contacts, and delayed alerts can create reassurance without making care feel intrusive or surveillance-like.

Research angle: lightweight care interaction →
Solo work · AI productivityiOS

Solo HQ

A one-person business management workspace that integrates tasks, projects, finance, and operating rhythm. It asks how AI-era independent creators manage work when the boundary between individual and organization becomes blurred.

Research angle: individual-as-organization →
Language learning · AI tutorPrototype

SoloSpeak

A scenario-based English learning system that maps user goals to routes, daily practice, vocabulary, speaking, listening, and review. It explores how AI can make learning contextual, goal-driven, and emotionally sustainable.

Research angle: situated AI learning →
