About

A hybrid profile across design, code, and HCI research.

I am Chen Ziyan, an interaction designer and independent developer with a background in computer science and information product innovation design. My work focuses on how AI can become more understandable, trustworthy, and useful in real contexts.

I studied Computer Science and Technology as an undergraduate and Innovative Design of Information Products as a graduate student. My training combines implementation logic, interaction design, usability evaluation, user experience metrics, and design research methods.

After working as an interaction designer in industry, I founded Zibuyu Studio to explore independent product building and the role of AI across the product design lifecycle—from requirement analysis and concept generation to prototyping, interface design, and product iteration.
