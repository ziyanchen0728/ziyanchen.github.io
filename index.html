Research Direction

From usable products to researchable questions.

My current research interests focus on how AI systems can become more situated, reflective, and supportive in everyday contexts, especially for learning, independent creation, and personal wellbeing.

01

Human-AI interaction for situated learning

How can AI adapt language learning to a user's real goals, contexts, routines, and emotional barriers rather than only generating generic exercises?

02

AI-supported independent work

How do solo creators and independent developers coordinate strategy, execution, finance, and reflection when AI tools change the boundary between individual and organization?

03

Reflective and emotional interaction design

How can digital systems support self-awareness, reassurance, and care without becoming intrusive, clinical, or overly quantified?

04

Design methods for AI-native products

How should designers prototype, evaluate, and communicate AI product experiences when system behavior is probabilistic, contextual, and continuously evolving?
